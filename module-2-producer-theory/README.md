# Module 2: Producer Theory

[← Microeconomics Simulator](../README.md)

Interactive teaching simulator for **Module 2: Producer Theory**. As the price rises, the firm expands output up to the point where marginal cost equals the price, and the trace of those points is the firm's supply curve. The lessons let you carry out derivations like this one step by step, with your own hands.

## What it covers

20 lessons that run from short-run production to long-run technology, on to costs, and from there to profit maximization and supply. Every lesson follows the same path: concepts, a worked example, interactive exploration, derivation, exercises and a summary.

- **Quick path:** each lesson offers a short route at the top for brief use in class.
- **Graduate layer:** a switch adds formulas and further results to the same graphs.
- **Introduction and user guide** video in English and Turkish.
