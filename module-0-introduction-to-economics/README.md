# Module 0: Introduction to Economics

[← Microeconomics Simulator](../README.md)

Interactive teaching simulator for **Module 0: Introduction to Economics**. The quantity buyers want and the quantity sellers offer are brought into line by the price. When demand changes, price and quantity move together to a new equilibrium. The lessons build the basic way economists think, and the supply and demand model, step by step, with your own hands.

## What it covers

13 lessons that start from scarcity and choice, move on to utility and marginal thinking, then to supply, demand and market equilibrium, and finish with elasticities, welfare and government intervention. Every lesson follows the same path: concepts, a worked example, interactive exploration, derivation, exercises and a summary.

- **Quick path:** each lesson offers a short route at the top for brief use in class.
- **Introduction and user guide** video in English and Turkish.
- **A starting point** for [Module 1: Consumer Theory](../module-1-consumer-theory/README.md) and [Module 2: Producer Theory](../module-2-producer-theory/README.md).

### Part D. Welfare and applications

### Part E. Government intervention
